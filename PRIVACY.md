# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 2 October 2025

Ferrot does not collect, share, or transmit any personal or sensitive user data.
All functionality runs locally on your device.

If you add cookies for a site, Ferrot stores them locally on your device so yt-dlp can use your
logged-in session for matching downloads. If you use Get cookies, the in-app WebView may also store
site cookies locally. These cookies are not uploaded or shared by Ferrot, and saved cookies can be
deleted from the Cookies settings screen.

Anonymous, non-identifiable usage data may be collected in some builds through Firebase Analytics to
help improve stability and performance.

If this ever changes, this document will be updated.
